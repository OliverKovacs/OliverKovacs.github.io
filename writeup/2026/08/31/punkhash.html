Problem #

Let \(p \in \mathbb P\) be prime, \(n \in \mathbb N\), and \(b \in \mathbb N\) with \(b \ll p\). Let \(v_1, \ldots, v_n \in_{\text{u.a.r.}} \{0, \ldots, p - 1\}\) and \(x_1, \ldots, x_n \in_{\text{u.a.r.}} \{0, \ldots, b\}\) be chosen uniformly at random. Define \(c \in \{0, \ldots, p - 1\}\) as \[ c = v_1 x_1 + \ldots + v_n x_n \mod{p}. \tag{1} \] Given \(p, b, v_1, \ldots, v_n, c\) determine \(x_1, \ldots, x_n\).

Solution #

Simple #

We rewrite equation (1) as

\[ v_1 x_1 + \cdots + v_n x_n + k p - c = 0 \tag{2} \]

for some unknown \(k \in \mathbb Z\). Now define the \((n + 2) \times (n + 2)\) matrix \(M\) over \(\mathbb Z\) as

\[ M = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & w \cdot v_1 \\ 0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & w \cdot v_2 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 0 & w \cdot v_n \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & w \cdot p \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & -1 & w \cdot c \\ \end{bmatrix} \]

where \(w \in \mathbb Z_{>0}\), and consider the vector \(x\) with

\[ x = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & \cdots & x_n & k & -1 \\ \end{bmatrix} . \]

Observe that for \(M\) and \(x\) we have

\[ \begin{align*} x M = &\begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_n & 1 & w \cdot (\sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i x_i + k p - c) \\ \end{bmatrix} \\ \stackrel{(2)}{=} &\begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_n & 1 & 0 \\ \end{bmatrix} \end{align*} \]

Since \(xM\) is a relatively short vector we apply the LLL lattice basis reduction algorithm to \(M\) and hope to find it as a row of the result. A suitable \(w\) has to be guessed.

Centered #

Should this not produce a result we can try to construct an even shorter vector.

Define \(\delta = \lfloor b/2 \rfloor\), define \(y_i = x_i - \delta\) for every \(i \in \{1, \ldots, n\}\), and define \(c' \in \mathbb Z\) as

\[ c' = c - \delta \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i. \]

Observe that

\[ \begin{align*} \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i y_i + k p - c' &= \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i (x_i - \delta) + k p - c' \\ &= \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i x_i - \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i \delta + k p - c + \delta \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i \\ &= \sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i x_i + k p - c \\ &\stackrel{(2)}{=} 0 . \tag{3} \end{align*} \]

Now define the \((n + 2) \times (n + 2)\) matrix \(M'\) over \(\mathbb Z\) as

\[ M' = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & w \cdot v_1 \\ 0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & w \cdot v_2 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 0 & w \cdot v_n \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & w \cdot p \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & -1 & w \cdot c' \\ \end{bmatrix} \]

where \(w \in \mathbb Z_{>0}\), and consider the vector \(y\) with

\[ y = \begin{bmatrix} y_1 & \cdots & y_n & k & -1 \\ \end{bmatrix} . \]

Observe that for \(M'\) and \(y\) we have

\[ \begin{align*} y M' = &\begin{bmatrix} y_1 & y_2 & \cdots & y_n & 1 & w \cdot (\sum_{i = 1}^{n} v_i y_i + k p - c') \\ \end{bmatrix} \\ \stackrel{(3)}{=} &\begin{bmatrix} y_1 & y_2 & \cdots & y_n & 1 & 0 \\ \end{bmatrix} \end{align*} \]

Since \(\lceil -b/2 \rceil \le y_i \le \lceil b / 2 \rceil\) whereas \(0 \le x_i \le b\) for every \(i \in \{1, \ldots, n\}\), \(y M'\) will usually be shorter than \(x M\). We apply the LLL lattice basis reduction algorithm to \(M'\) and hope to find \(y M'\) as a row in the result. We can then trivially reconstruct \(x_1, \ldots, x_n\).

Implementation #

#!/usr/bin/env sage

def solve_simple(p, n, b, v, c):

    # construct M
    w = 2 ** ((n // 2) + 5) * b
    M = Matrix(ZZ, n + 2, n + 2)
    for i in range(n):
        M[i,i] = 1
        M[i,n+1] = w * v[i]
    M[n,n+1] = w * p
    M[n+1,n] = -1
    M[n+1,n+1] = w * c

    L = M.LLL()

    # check for potential solution
    for row in L:
        if not (row[n+1] == 0 and abs(row[n]) == 1):
            continue

        if row[n] == -1:
            row *= -1

        x = row[:n]
        if not all(0 <= xi <= b for xi in x):
            continue

        if (sum(vi * xi for vi, xi in zip(v, x)) - c) % p:
            continue

        return list(x)

    return None

def solve_centered(p, n, b, v, c):

    # construct M_prime
    w = 2 ** ((n // 2) + 5) * b
    delta = b // 2
    c_prime = c - delta * sum(v)
    M_prime = Matrix(ZZ, n + 2, n + 2)
    for i in range(n):
        M_prime[i,i]   = 1
        M_prime[i,n+1] = w * v[i]
    M_prime[n,n+1]   = w * p
    M_prime[n+1,n]   = -1
    M_prime[n+1,n+1] = w * c_prime

    L = M_prime.LLL()

    # check for potential solution
    for row in L:
        if not (row[n+1] == 0 and abs(row[n]) == 1):
            continue

        if row[n] == -1:
            row *= -1

        y = row[:n]
        x = [yi + delta for yi in y]

        if not all(0 <= xi <= b for xi in x):
            continue

        if (sum(vi * xi for vi, xi in zip(v, x)) - c) % p:
            continue

        return list(x)

    return None

from random import randint

p = 161435094849208186723743776068899835990543
n = 16
b = 255
v = [randint(0, p - 1) for _ in range(n)]
x = [randint(0, b) for _ in range(n)]
c = sum([vi * xi % p for vi, xi in zip(v, x)]) % p

print("# problem")
print(f"p = {p}")
print(f"n = {n}")
print(f"b = {b}")
print(f"v = {v}")
print(f"c = {c}")

print()
print("# solution")
print(f"x = {x}")

X = solve_centered(p, n, b, v, c)

print()
